Not a medical device. StrokeSight is a machine-learning decision-support prototype built for an academic internship project (Intel® Unnati — AI for Medical Intelligence). It estimates statistical risk patterns from historical data and does not diagnose stroke or any medical condition. Always consult a qualified physician for medical concerns.
